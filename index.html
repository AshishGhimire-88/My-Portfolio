About Me

Hello! I'm Ashish Ghimire, an 18-year-old undergraduate student currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Data Science at the University of Texas at Arlington. I'm a rising sophmore, expecting to graduate in May 2029. I completed my high school education at Trinity International Secondary School, Nepal, majoring in science with a focus on computer science and mathematics.

I discovered the field of data science during my Grade 11 classes while learning about Big Data. What drew me in was how the field beautifully combines subjects I've always enjoyed—calculus, probability, statistics, and programming. Since then, I've developed a deep interest in data analysis, machine learning, and the entire data science pipeline.

I am determined to pursue a career as a reliable and skilled machine learning engineer. I'm actively working to build strong foundations by engaging in self-study and hands-on projects. With consistent effort and curiosity, I aim to contribute meaningfully to the world of intelligent systems and data-driven solutions.
